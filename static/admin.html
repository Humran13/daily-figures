Clean ledger cutover

A cutover establishes a new, verified operational starting point for every active product's Opening Stock without deleting or rewriting historical data. Pre-cutover records remain visible for reference but never contribute to post-cutover stock once a cutover is activated. Workflow: draft → every active product gets a verified physical-count balance → verify → activate (requires a typed confirmation phrase and explicit backup confirmation).
